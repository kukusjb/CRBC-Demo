What is Good News Club?

A mission field at the school down the road

Not every family who could hear the gospel will ever walk through our doors. Good News Club meets kids where they already are — bringing dynamic Bible teaching, joyful music, and genuine care right onto a school campus, one afternoon a week.

📖

Bible Lessons That Point to Jesus

Every lesson is grounded in Scripture and taught so kids leave knowing not just a story, but how it points to who Jesus is and what He's done.

🎵

Songs & Missionary Stories

Energetic music and true stories from missionaries around the world help kids see themselves as part of a much bigger story.

🏅

Scripture Memory & Prizes

Kids memorize a verse each week and are celebrated for it — hiding God's Word in their hearts becomes something to look forward to.

🤝

Open to Any Child

No church background required. Good News Club is free and welcomes any student with a signed permission form, whatever their family's faith background.

Who runs it

Sponsored by Child Evangelism Fellowship®

Good News Club is a program of Child Evangelism Fellowship (CEF), a ministry that has been reaching children with the gospel for decades. CRBC partners with CEF to bring an official Good News Club to Killearn Lakes Elementary, led by trained and background-screened volunteers from our church.

Because it meets on school property immediately after the final bell, Good News Club reaches kids who may never otherwise hear the gospel — with full compliance with school policies and parental permission for every child who attends.

How it works

A few things every parent or volunteer should know about how Good News Club runs at Killearn Lakes Elementary.

📝
A signed permission form is required for every child before their first club
🛡
Every volunteer is background-screened and trained by CEF
🗓
Meets weekly during the school year, following the Leon County Schools calendar for holidays
💵
Completely free — there is never a cost to attend
